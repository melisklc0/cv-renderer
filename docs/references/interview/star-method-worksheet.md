# Behavioral Interviews — STAR Method Worksheet

Behavioral interviewing is a technique used by employers in which the questions asked assist
the employer in making predictions about a potential employee's future success based on past
behaviors. In behavior-based interviews, candidates are asked to give specific examples of
when they demonstrated particular behaviors or skills.

## Effective Formula for Answering Behavioral Interviews

- **S:** Describe the **S**ituation you were in
- **T:** Describe the **T**ask you needed to accomplish
- **A:** Describe the **A**ction you took
- **R:** Describe the **R**esults of your experience
