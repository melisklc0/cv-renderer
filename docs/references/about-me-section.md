# What is an About Me section in a CV?

Typically, you divide your CV into sections that document your academic and professional career chronologically. One of these sections has the title about me and allows you to present concise details about your area of expertise, strengths and professional accomplishments to date. This section often describes to employers what makes you an ideal candidate and provides them with convincing reasons to hire you.

In addition to your CV, about me sections are often used in other important documents pertaining to your employment applications, such as in your cover letters or on your professional online profile. Recruiters and recruitment managers consider the details you include in these areas with as much weight as this section in your CV, so be sure to give it as much thought and follow the same 'title, skills and goals.' structure. These sections in these areas can be less succinct and can allow you to expand into a slightly more descriptive professional biography.

## How to write an About Me section in a CV

Well-crafted about me statements can help employers form a connection with you. However, creating a confident endorsement of yourself can sometimes feel challenging. You can follow these steps to help you learn how to write an about me section in your CV that's effective:

### 1. List professional titles

First, convey your professional credentials to the reader; use words such as senior or qualified where appropriate to deliver a sense of professional competence or experience in your role. If you have an established career in a related work industry, be certain to include your full titles, such as lead technical advisor or senior accounts manager. In the case where you may be applying for an opportunity where you have had little or no professional experience, you could choose titles such as entry-level administrator or honours degree graduate of science.

### 2. Provide relevant skills and accomplishments

It's important to quickly establish your capability to handle the role you're applying for. You can highlight skills that directly affect the position and list examples of relevant professional accomplishments to help outline how qualified you are. You could benefit from highlighting the characteristics that make you the best candidate for the position you're applying for. Describe your relevant skills and accomplishments that could make you stand out from other candidates.

### 3. Add career goals

A short note to illustrate your professional ambitions in this section is a great way to impart a sense of your drive and commitment to excel in the sector of work you're involved in. Conclude this section with a statement about your short-term and long-term career goals. By expressing your conviction about your work ambitions, recruitment managers can gauge your determination to become a successful professional and recognise your dedication to excelling in the role you're applying for.

## Benefits of an About Me CV section

This section serves as vital information adverts for your professional prospects and ambitions. You can yield many benefits from including this paragraph in your CV. Some of these benefits include:

- **It serves to summarise.** This section provides a summary of your whole curriculum vitae in one section. This summation acts as an attention-grabbing headline in which you can abbreviate the details of your entire CV in a shorthand format.
- **A space to brand yourself.** This section also provides you with an opportunity to brand yourself professionally. By using carefully chosen words to positively describe your skills and achievements, you can portray a style or brand that you believe best represents your character.
- **It quantifies your experience.** When this introductory section is successfully completed, it can help employers quantify your experience. The level of experience and success of your accomplishments listed here allows readers to quickly place a quantifiable value on your worth as a company asset.

## Tips for writing an About Me CV section

These additional tips may help you complete the about me section in your CV:

- **Be brief:** It's important not to over-elaborate in this section. Keep your statements short and to the point, and use specific instances that clearly illustrate relevant strengths or accomplishments.
- **Be truthful:** Avoid any forms of embellishment or exaggeration in this section. If you don't have relevant work experience, you can list personal skills, strengths or knowledge you gained through education or voluntary work.
- **Proofread and read aloud:** To ensure the copy sounds perfect, read it aloud a couple of times. This helps you identify any grammatical or structural errors and hone the readability of the section.
- **Keep the section updated:** As your career advances and you gain work experience, it's important that this section reflects these developments and showcases them to a high standard. Be sure to review this paragraph as your occupations and experiences change.
- **Tie it into the job description:** Different roles require different abilities, so remember to tailor this section accordingly. This may involve tweaking the existing copy or adding keywords to make it more appropriate for the role and more lucrative to the firm you're applying to.

## Professional profile examples

Researching what other professionals have included in their own about me sections can be helpful. These are a few examples of opening introductions written for individuals in various industries:

**College student**

An honours graduate with a bachelor's degree in commerce and finance keen to gain industry experience in the banking sector. A highly organised individual who academically excelled in the areas of market forecasts and corporate accounting. Committed to creating a career in finance, with long-term ambitions to become a corporate advisor.

**Sales representative**

Floor sales assistant for a reputable car dealership just outside the city. Outgoing and dedicated to customer satisfaction, driving steady increases in company sales revenue over a three-year period. Brings in-depth knowledge of automobiles from prior work as a mechanic's apprentice, invaluable for assuring customers of quality purchases. Aims to bring this passion for cars and customer satisfaction into senior management roles in the car-sales industry.

**Office manager**

Highly competent office administrator with five years of experience providing administrative support for the office manager. Methodical and organised, with proficiency across scheduling and communication software tools. Brings a track record of reliable execution and enthusiasm for excelling in administration to office management roles.

**Advertising agent**

Multimedia manager at a small city-centre design house, following four years of design and marketing study and a graphic design internship. Focused on creating successful advertising strategies and developing winning brand campaigns for clients. Aims to secure a role at a prominent advertising agency, working with leading brand names.

**Career change**

English teacher for the past ten years, now expanding into copywriting and translation for a multinational marketing firm. Brings a degree in English and fluency in Spanish alongside a strong command of language. Open to relocation for the right opportunity.
